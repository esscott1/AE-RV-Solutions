## Q: Do I need to be parked in the sun for solar to work?

Solar still charges in shade and cloudy weather, just much more slowly:
often 10–25% of full output. If you camp under trees a lot, a larger
battery bank matters more than extra panels.

## Q: Will solar charge my batteries while I'm driving?

Yes. Roof panels charge whenever there's daylight, parked or moving.
