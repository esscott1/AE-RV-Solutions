## What it takes

A typical 1,000 W RV microwave draws about 1,500 W from the inverter while
it's running. You need:

- An inverter of at least 2,000 W continuous (pure sine wave).
- A battery bank that can deliver that power: roughly 200 Ah of lithium at
  12 V for a few short uses a day.
- Enough solar to put the energy back: 400 W or more in sunny weather.

Five minutes of microwaving uses about 125 Wh, roughly the same as running
a laptop for three hours.

## How A&E sets it up

We size the inverter, battery bank, and solar together from how you
actually cook, so the microwave runs without the generator and the
batteries are full again by the afternoon. Everything is installed,
fused, and tested by a technician.
