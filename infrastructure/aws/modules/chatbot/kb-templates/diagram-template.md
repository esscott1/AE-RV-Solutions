## What it shows

How a 400 Ah, 12 V lithium battery bank feeds a 3,000 W inverter/charger,
with solar charging from 800 W of roof panels through an MPPT charge
controller.

## Main components and ratings

- Batteries: 4 × 100 Ah 12 V lithium (LiFePO4) in parallel, 400 Ah total.
- Inverter/charger: 3,000 W continuous, pure sine wave.
- Solar: 4 × 200 W panels, 40 A MPPT charge controller.
- Main fuse and battery disconnect between the bank and the inverter.

## When this setup fits

Weekend-to-week trips off-grid with a microwave, coffee maker, laptops, and
a TV; air conditioning only for short periods.
