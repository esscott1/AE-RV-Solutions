# Capability: Run a microwave off-grid

## What it takes

A 3,000 watt inverter with a battery bank that can deliver 3,000 watt-hours
of power. Ask A&E about battery bank recommendations.

## How A&E sets it up

Wiring depends on your battery bank, but it is straightforward. Most
importantly, and too often overlooked, is installing cutoff switches and/or
breaker switches. Ask A&E about wiring.

## Also possible with this setup

You can also run a coffee maker and have all the plugs in your RV work, not
just the ones the manufacturer said are "inverter prepped". Ask A&E about
making your RV just like home when boondocking.
